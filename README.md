# CitadelleDuChaos

A [libGDX](https://libgdx.com/) project generated with [gdx-liftoff](https://github.com/libgdx/gdx-liftoff).

This project was generated with a template including simple application launchers and an `ApplicationAdapter` extension that draws libGDX logo.

## Synopsis
Hidden within the Citadel of Chaos, the fearsome wizard Balthus the Dire is preparing the invasion of the Valley of Willows. The attack is imminent.
Now, you alone are capable of answering the desperate call of King Solomon, who reigns over the Valley. 
Because you are the best student of the Grand Enchanter of Yore and a master in magic. 
But will you be able to penetrate within the Chaos Citadel and defeat Balthus in his sanctuary?

## About this project
A personnal project based on a "choose your own adventure" I read as a teenager. Will you be better than me to defeat Balthus ?
A great challenge about Oriented-Object coding.
